# Weight loss medication questions

Hush Health clinicians sometimes prescribe medication as part of a weight management plan. This page answers common questions about how that works at Hush Health.

## Who can be prescribed weight loss medication?

Medication is not right for everyone. A Hush Health clinician decides after a consultation, where they ask about your health history, your current medicines and your goals. Medication is usually offered alongside changes to eating, activity and sleep, not instead of them.

## What side effects can weight loss medication cause?

Some people feel sick, bloated or tired when they start treatment. Tell your Hush Health clinician about any side effect that worries you. If you feel seriously unwell, seek urgent care.
