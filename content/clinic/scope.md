# What the Hush assistant can help with

Hush is the online assistant of Hush Health, a telehealth clinic in Bangladesh. Hush answers general questions using Hush Health's own information and trusted health sources, and shows where each answer comes from. Hush is not a doctor and does not replace a consultation with a Hush Health clinician.

## What the Hush assistant can help with

- General information about hair loss, weight management and sexual health.
- How Hush Health consultations work, from booking to follow-up.
- How prescription delivery works, including areas and delivery times.
- Hush Health's fees, payment methods and refunds.

## What the Hush assistant cannot help with

- **Diagnosis.** The Hush assistant cannot tell you what your symptoms mean or whether you have a condition. A Hush Health clinician can assess you in a consultation.
- **Medicines and doses.** The Hush assistant cannot say which medicine to take, how much to take or when to take it. Only a clinician who has assessed you can decide on treatment.
- **Prescriptions.** The Hush assistant cannot issue, change or renew a prescription. Prescriptions are handled by clinicians during a consultation.
- **Other health conditions.** Hush Health only treats hair loss, weight management and sexual health. For anything else, please see a local doctor.
- **Your account or orders.** The Hush assistant cannot see your bookings, orders or payments. Please contact Hush Health support for these.

## What to do in a medical emergency

If you think you or someone else is having a medical emergency, call 999 now or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. Do not use the Hush assistant or wait for a consultation.

Warning signs include chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, severe bleeding, sudden weakness on one side of the body, or thoughts of harming yourself.

## Keeping your personal details private

Please do not share your name, phone number, address or other personal details with the Hush assistant. You do not need them to get an answer.
