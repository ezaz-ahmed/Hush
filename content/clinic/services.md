# Hush Health services

Hush Health offers three services: hair loss, weight management and sexual health. Each service starts with a private online consultation with a licensed clinician, who assesses your situation and discusses your options with you.

## Hair loss service at Hush Health

The hair loss service is for adults who are noticing thinning hair or hair loss. A clinician reviews your health history and photos of your scalp. Depending on what they find, the clinician may recommend treatment, suggest changes that can help, or refer you to an in-person specialist. Hush Health cannot promise that treatment will stop or reverse hair loss, because results differ from person to person.

## Weight management service at Hush Health

The weight management service is for adults who want support to reach and keep a healthier weight. A clinician reviews your health history, current weight and goals, then agrees a plan with you. The plan may cover eating, physical activity and, where the clinician decides it is suitable, prescription treatment. Regular follow-up consultations check your progress and adjust the plan. Hush Health does not promise a set amount of weight loss.

## Sexual health service at Hush Health

The sexual health service is for adults with concerns such as erection problems, low sexual desire or questions about sexually transmitted infections. Consultations are confidential and handled without judgement. The clinician may recommend treatment or refer you to a local clinic or laboratory for tests, because Hush Health cannot take samples online.

## When Hush Health refers you elsewhere

Some care cannot be given online. A Hush Health clinician will refer you to a local doctor, clinic or hospital when you need a physical examination or tests, or when your concern is outside hair loss, weight management and sexual health. In a medical emergency, do not wait for a consultation: call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
