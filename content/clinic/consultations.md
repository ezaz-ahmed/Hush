# How Hush Health consultations work

A Hush Health consultation has five steps, and all of them happen online: booking, a health questionnaire, the video consultation, a written summary, and follow-up if needed.

## Booking a Hush Health consultation

Book through the Hush Health website or app. Choose the service you need (hair loss, weight management or sexual health), then pick a time slot between 9am and 9pm, Saturday to Thursday. You pay the consultation fee when you book. The pricing FAQ lists the fees.

## The health questionnaire before your consultation

After booking, you fill in a short health questionnaire. It asks about your health history, any medicines you currently take and any allergies, so the clinician can prepare before your consultation. For the hair loss service, you are also asked to upload photos of your scalp. Only your clinician and the Hush Health care team can see your answers.

## The Hush Health video consultation

The consultation is a video call with a licensed clinician and usually lasts 15 to 20 minutes. You need a phone or computer with a camera and a quiet, private place. The clinician asks about your concern, goes through your questionnaire and discusses your options with you. If your internet connection is slow, you can ask for an audio-only call instead.

## After your Hush Health consultation

After the call, a written summary of your consultation appears in your Hush Health account. If the clinician prescribes treatment, the prescription is sent to the Hush Health pharmacy and delivered to you. If the clinician refers you elsewhere, the summary includes a referral letter to take to a local doctor, clinic or laboratory.

## Follow-up consultations at Hush Health

Your clinician tells you if you need a follow-up consultation and when. Book follow-ups the same way as your first consultation, through the website or app. You can change or cancel a booking in your account; the pricing FAQ explains when you get a refund.
