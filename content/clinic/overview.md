# About Hush Health

Hush Health is an online telehealth clinic in Bangladesh. It offers private consultations with licensed clinicians for hair loss, weight management and sexual health. Everything happens online, from booking to follow-up, so patients can get care discreetly from home.

## Where Hush Health operates

Hush Health is an online-only clinic and has no walk-in location. Patients anywhere in Bangladesh can book a consultation. Patients must be 18 or older.

## Hush Health opening hours

Clinicians hold video consultations from 9am to 9pm, Saturday to Thursday. Hush Health is closed on Fridays and on public holidays.

The Hush assistant answers general questions at any time, including when the clinic is closed. It is not for emergencies: in a medical emergency, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## How to contact Hush Health support

For questions about your account, bookings, orders or payments, send a message to Hush Health support through the Hush Health website or app. Support replies within one working day.

## Privacy at Hush Health

Consultations with Hush Health clinicians are confidential. Hush Health only asks for the personal details it needs to provide care.
