# Hush Health pricing FAQ

All prices are in Bangladeshi taka (৳).

## How much does a Hush Health consultation cost?

A first consultation costs ৳1,000 for every service: hair loss, weight management and sexual health. You pay when you book.

## How much does a Hush Health follow-up consultation cost?

A follow-up consultation costs ৳500. You pay when you book it.

## Is medicine included in the Hush Health consultation fee?

No. Medicine is priced separately from the consultation. If a clinician prescribes treatment, the price is shown in your Hush Health account before you confirm the order.

## How much does Hush Health delivery cost?

Delivery costs ৳60 in Dhaka and ৳120 in other areas of Bangladesh.

## How can I pay Hush Health?

You can pay with a debit or credit card or through mobile financial services. For medicine orders, you can also pay cash on delivery.

## Can I get a refund if I cancel a Hush Health consultation?

If you cancel at least 24 hours before your consultation, you get a full refund. If you cancel later than that, or miss the consultation, the fee is not refunded. If Hush Health cancels your consultation, you always get a full refund.

## What happens if my Hush Health order arrives damaged or wrong?

Do not use the item. Send a message to Hush Health support through the website or app, and Hush Health will send a replacement at no cost, including delivery.
