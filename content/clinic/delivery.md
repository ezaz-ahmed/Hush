# Hush Health prescription delivery

When a Hush Health clinician prescribes treatment, the Hush Health pharmacy prepares your order and delivers it to your door. Delivery is available anywhere in Bangladesh.

## Where Hush Health delivers

Hush Health delivers to all 64 districts of Bangladesh. You can have your order delivered to your home or to another address, such as your workplace. You choose the delivery address in your Hush Health account.

## How long Hush Health delivery takes

A pharmacist checks every prescription before the order is sent. After that, delivery usually takes 1 to 2 working days in Dhaka and 3 to 5 working days in other areas. Fridays and public holidays are not working days.

## Discreet packaging for Hush Health orders

Every order arrives in a plain package. The outside does not show the Hush Health name or describe what is inside.

## Tracking your Hush Health order

You can track your order in your Hush Health account. If your order has not arrived within the expected time, send a message to Hush Health support through the website or app.

## Problems with your Hush Health delivery

If your order arrives damaged, opened, or with an item you do not recognise, do not use it. Send a message to Hush Health support through the website or app, and Hush Health will send a replacement. The pricing FAQ explains the delivery fee.
